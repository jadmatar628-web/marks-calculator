Rules

  • Marks must be whole numbers.
  • Partial marks are entered over 30.
  • Final exam marks are entered over 70 and shown independently.
  • Course grades are entered over 100 and use the pass, compensation, GPA, and fail rules.
  • A course passes with a mark of 50 or higher.
  • If the weighted average is 55 or higher, a course with 40 to 49 is compensation.
  • Any course below 40 is a fail.
  • GPA is calculated from the credit-weighted semester average.
  • LU average 50-63% corresponds to GPA 2.20-3.02.
  • LU average 63-72% corresponds to GPA 3.02-3.63.
  • LU average 72-80% corresponds to GPA 3.63-4.00.
  • Averages of 80% or higher are shown as 4.00.
  • GPA values between published boundaries are estimated proportionally.
